🩺 Mod Health

⚠️ Mod Health is still under development

This panel reports a lot, and a good number of its reports are false positives — content that is perfectly fine in game. Treat what you see here as leads to check, not a defect list.

Auto-fix is not ready. It writes to your mod's files, and when it acts on a report that was wrong it can change or remove content that was never broken. We recommend you do not use it. If you do, back up your mod first — every other maker in Nova is safe to use; this one is the exception.

The rules are being rewritten mod by mod. Reports of a rule that cries wolf are very welcome — they are exactly what the work needs.

Scans your active mod and reports content that won't work in-game — duplicate IDs, missing localization, national spirits nothing grants, and references to things that don't exist.
